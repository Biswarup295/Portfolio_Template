01 — About

Design instincts, engineering discipline.

I started out making tiny websites for friends and never stopped. Today I work across the stack, but my centre of gravity is the front end: the point where design decisions become milliseconds, layout and feel.

My approach is simple. Prototype early, keep the platform close, and let motion explain structure instead of decorating it. The result is interfaces that stay fast on a mid-range phone and still feel crafted on a large display.

  • ◆ Performance budgets treated as a feature, not an afterthought.
  • ◆ Accessible by default: keyboard paths, semantics and contrast.
  • ◆ Design systems that teams can actually keep using.
